# PulmoVision AI — Thoracic CT Dataset Analysis & Benchmark Phantoms (`DATASET_DOCUMENTATION.md`)

## 1. Public Thoracic CT Dataset Evaluation

In accordance with Section 6 of the Master Project Specification, we evaluated major public chest CT datasets to verify what each dataset actually annotates and prevent conflating nodule coordinates or automated masks with radiologist-validated anatomical masks:

| Dataset | Modality | Primary Annotations | Suitability for Anatomical Lung Segmentation |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **LCTSC (Lung CT Segmentation Challenge — AAPM 2017)** | Chest CT (DICOM + RTSTRUCT, 60 scans) | Expert radiation-oncology contours for **Left Lung**, **Right Lung**, Spinal Cord, Esophagus, and Heart. | **Ideal** reference standard for bilateral whole-lung segmentation evaluation. |
| **LUNA16 (Lung Nodule Analysis 2016)** | Chest CT (`mhd/raw`, 888 scans) | Centroid coordinates + diameters of pulmonary nodules ($\ge 3\text{ mm}$), plus auxiliary lung masks originally generated by an automated region-growing algorithm. | **Caution:** LUNA16 lung masks are algorithmically generated (often failing in dense consolidation), not manual radiologist references. Nodule centroids are point coordinates, not voxel masks. |
| **LIDC-IDRI** | Chest CT (DICOM + XML, 1,018 scans) | 4-radiologist XML polygon outlines for **pulmonary nodules**, not whole-lung parenchyma. | Suitable for Phase C nodule research; does **not** provide whole-lung parenchyma labels. |
| **Medical Segmentation Decathlon (MSD) Task06_Lung** | Chest CT (NIfTI, 63 training scans) | Voxel-level **NSCLC lung tumor** masks (`label=1`). | Annotates lung tumors only, not anatomical left/right lung parenchyma. |
| **VISCERAL / R231 Training Cohort (Hofmanninger et al., 2020)** | Routine Chest CT (231 diverse clinical scans) | Manual radiologist-verified bilateral and lobar lung masks across severe pathologies. | Source training cohort for the integrated `lungmask_r231` model. |

## 2. Included Calibrated Thoracic CT Benchmark Datasets (`sample_data/`)

To enable deterministic local testing, CI verification, and immediate interactive demonstration without redistributing multi-gigabyte patient archives, `backend/app/ml/phantom_generator.py` synthesizes radiometrically calibrated 3D Thoracic CT volumes in Hounsfield Units (`Int16`):

* **Anatomical & Radiometric Features:**
  * Extracorporeal scanner air: $-1000\text{ HU}$
  * Thoracic soft-tissue wall ($+45\text{ HU}$), subcutaneous adipose rim ($-70\text{ HU}$), and posterior CT table ($+180\text{ HU}$)
  * Cortical vertebral body, spinal canal, spinous processes, and periodic rib cage ($+520\text{ HU}$ to $+610\text{ HU}$)
  * Mediastinum and cardiac notch silhouette ($+45\text{ HU}$)
  * Trachea lumen ($-995\text{ HU}$) and tracheal cartilage wall ($+65\text{ HU}$)
  * Bilateral lung parenchyma (**Right Lung = Label 1**, **Left Lung = Label 2**) with supine anteroposterior gravity gradient, vascular branching texture ($-825\text{ HU} \pm 45\text{ HU}$), interlobar fissures ($-640\text{ HU}$), and quantum noise ($\sigma = 14\text{ HU}$).

### Generated Files in `sample_data/`
1. `pulmovision_chest_ct_48slices.nii.gz` ($48 \times 256 \times 256$, spacing $1.25 \times 1.25 \times 2.5\text{ mm}$)
2. `pulmovision_chest_ct_axial_dicom.zip` (48-slice Axial DICOM series with intentionally shuffled filenames/InstanceNumbers)
3. `pulmovision_chest_ct_oblique_12deg_dicom.zip` (48-slice Oblique DICOM series acquired at a $12^\circ$ gantry tilt with shuffled filenames)
