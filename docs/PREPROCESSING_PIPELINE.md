## 1. Oblique-Aware DICOM Series Validation & Sorting

Sorting DICOM files by filename, `InstanceNumber` `(0020,0013)`, or raw $Z$-coordinate `ImagePositionPatient[2]` fails when a CT scan is acquired with gantry tilt or oblique orientation. PulmoVision AI implements full 3D vector projection sorting:

1. **Series Grouping & Coplanarity Check:**
   * Groups candidate DICOM slices by `SeriesInstanceUID` `(0020,000E)` and selects the primary volumetric CT series.
   * Extracts the reference row direction cosine vector $\mathbf{r}_0 = [r_x, r_y, r_z]^T$ and column direction cosine vector $\mathbf{c}_0 = [c_x, c_y, c_z]^T$ from `ImageOrientationPatient` `(0020,0037)`.
   * Verifies coplanarity for every slice $i$:
     $$\mathbf{r}_i \cdot \mathbf{r}_0 \ge 0.999 \quad \text{and} \quad \mathbf{c}_i \cdot \mathbf{c}_0 \ge 0.999$$
   * Verifies consistent `Rows`, `Columns`, and `PixelSpacing` `(0028,0030)` across the entire series.
2. **Right-Handed Normal Vector & Scalar Projection:**
   * Computes the unit slice normal vector:
     $$\mathbf{n} = \frac{\mathbf{r}_0 \times \mathbf{c}_0}{\|\mathbf{r}_0 \times \mathbf{c}_0\|_2}$$
   * Projects each slice's `ImagePositionPatient` `(0020,0032)` $\mathbf{p}_i = [p_{x,i}, p_{y,i}, p_{z,i}]^T$ onto $\mathbf{n}$:
     $$d_i = \mathbf{p}_i \cdot \mathbf{n}$$
   * Sorts slices in strictly ascending order of $d_i$.
3. **Inter-Slice Gap & Uniformity Verification:**
   * Deduplicates slices with identical `SOPInstanceUID` or $\Delta d < 10^{-3}\text{ mm}$.
   * Computes inter-slice distances $\Delta d_k = d_{(k+1)} - d_{(k)}$ and median spacing $\Delta z = \text{median}(\Delta d_k)$.
   * Raises `NonUniformSliceSpacingError` (`HTTP 422`) if $\max_k |\Delta d_k - \Delta z| > \max(0.15 \cdot \Delta z,\ 0.5\text{ mm})$.
4. **Hounsfield Unit (HU) Calibration:**
   * Converts raw stored pixel values $V_{\text{raw}}(z, y, x)$ using per-slice `RescaleSlope` $m_z$ `(0028,1053)` and `RescaleIntercept` $b_z$ `(0028,1052)`:
     $$\text{HU}(z, y, x) = \text{clip}\left(\text{round}(V_{\text{raw}}(z, y, x) \cdot m_z + b_z),\ -1024,\ 4096\right) \in \text{int16}$$
   * Scanner outside-FOV circular padding values ($<-2048$) are clamped to $-1024\text{ HU}$ (air).

## 2. Canonical LPS+ Coordinate System Strategy

To eliminate left/right or superior/inferior mirroring discrepancies between NIfTI (RAS+) and DICOM (LPS+):

1. **Dual-Geometry Storage:**
   * **`native_image` (`volume_native.nii.gz`):** Preserves the exact acquisition `Origin`, `Spacing`, and $3 \times 3$ `Direction` cosine matrix $[\mathbf{r}_0, \mathbf{c}_0, \mathbf{n}]$.
   * **`lps_image` (`volume_lps_int16.npy`):** Reoriented via `SimpleITK.DICOMOrient(native_image, "LPS")` into canonical axis-aligned **LPS+**:
     * $+X$ index ($0 \to X-1$): Patient Right $\to$ Patient Left
     * $+Y$ index ($0 \to Y-1$): Anterior (chest) $\to$ Posterior (back)
     * $+Z$ index ($0 \to Z-1$): Inferior (feet) $\to$ Superior (head)
2. **Radiological MPR Slice Orientation (`backend/app/api/viewer.py`):**
   * **Axial (`plane=axial`):** `vol_zyx[z, :, :]` — Top = Anterior, Bottom = Posterior, Screen Left = Patient Right, Screen Right = Patient Left.
   * **Coronal (`plane=coronal`):** `vol_zyx[::-1, y, :]` — Top = Superior (head up), Bottom = Inferior, Screen Left = Patient Right, Screen Right = Patient Left.
   * **Sagittal (`plane=sagittal`):** `vol_zyx[::-1, :, x]` — Top = Superior (head up), Bottom = Inferior, Screen Left = Anterior, Screen Right = Posterior.
3. **Native Spatial Restoration on Export:**
   * Segmentation masks generated by `LungMaskAdapter` are reoriented back to `native_orientation_code`, assigned `mask_sitk.CopyInformation(native_image)`, and verified with `np.allclose` assertions on `Spacing`, `Origin`, and `Direction` before saving.
